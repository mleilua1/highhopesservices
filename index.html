Helping Others Help Themselves

Our mission is to deliver exceptional services that enhance the quality of life for individuals of all ages, including those with autism, intellectual disabilities, and other developmental challenges, as well as children and youth facing mental health conditions, and those in the state's care.

About High Hopes Services

Our Program Philosophy

We believe that every family needs basic social skills combined with love to guide their children to be responsible and productive citizens. When a client is temporarily separated from their home, we utilize nation-wide research to provide specialized intervention, helping families stay securely connected and supported.

Our Long-Term Commitment

We help troubled clients and their families navigate critical adolescent years, providing the stable decision-making frameworks necessary to construct a successful, fulfilling, and well-rounded future in the community.

HHS Professional Parent & Host Home Programs

We provide truly individualized support outside of a traditional group environment along the Wasatch Front, offering safe, long-term residential programs backed by professional parents and highly trained staff.

Developmental Support

Tailored care for children, youth, and adults with Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD), Intellectual Disabilities, Developmental Delays, or Cognitive Disorders.

Mental Health Care

Specialized programs explicitly addressing youth with distinct mental health needs, behavioral challenges, or dual diagnoses.

State Custody Placements

Comprehensive proctoring, intensive supervision (tracking), and wrap-around care for individuals in DCFS, DSPD, and DJJS custody.
